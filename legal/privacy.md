# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## What we collect

When you use CATS, we collect:

- **Account data** — name, email address, age, preferred language
- **Health context** — pain areas, mobility level, lifestyle notes (entered during onboarding)
- **Session data** — exercise activity, rep counts, posture scores, session duration, pain log entries
- **Device data** — browser type, operating system, device identifiers (for analytics and error tracking)

## Camera and pose data

CATS uses your camera for real-time pose estimation during exercise sessions.

- **No raw video is recorded or stored.** The camera feed is processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning.
- Only derived data (joint angle measurements, posture scores, rep counts) is saved to your account.
- Camera data never leaves your device in its raw form.

## What we do not collect

- Raw camera footage
- Facial recognition data
- Medical records
- Payment information (unless explicitly added in future billing features)

## How we use your data

- To generate and personalise your rehabilitation plan
- To track your exercise progress over time
- To give your physiotherapist visibility into your sessions (if applicable)
- To improve platform accuracy and exercise recommendations

## Data sharing

We do not sell your data.

We share data only with:
- **Supabase** — our database and authentication provider
- **Anthropic** — for AI plan generation (only anonymised health context is sent; no personal identifiers)
- Your assigned physiotherapist, if you are part of a supervised plan

## Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

## Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is restricted by row-level security policies. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at the address below.
